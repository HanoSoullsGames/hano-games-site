About me

I am an indie developer from Belarus. I have loved playing games since childhood and decided to contribute to gamedev with my own creations. I make games in Unity: I design mechanics, write code, build the UI and take the project all the way to a build.

I love different genres. I try to make games with interesting mechanics or eye-catching visuals. I commission art from artists — my job is to make everything work and come together into a single picture, so that you enjoy spending your time with it.

There is one game in the portfolio so far, but it is only the beginning.
